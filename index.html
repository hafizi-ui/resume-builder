Resume or CV?

Choose Resume for internships and industry jobs: 1–2 pages, focused on skills and measurable results. Choose CV for further study, scholarships and research or academic posts: a complete record of education, research, teaching, publications, grants and references. Both use the same details; the CV adds section 10.

1. Fill in your details

On Fill-In Form, type into the yellow boxes. The grey tip under each box shows an example. Required boxes, including email, are marked *.

2. Check your resume

Open Resume to see the full A4 page. Then open Resume Coach and aim for a score of 80 or more before you send it.

3. Photo (optional)

Photos are off by default because many applicant tracking systems (ATS) cannot read them. If an employer asks for one, click Upload photo.

4. Extra slots

A 2nd job, a 3rd leadership role and a 4th achievement appear on the resume as soon as you fill them in. Empty lines are always hidden.

5. Download

Download PDF gives a one-page A4 file that recruiters and ATS systems can read. Download Word gives an editable .docx file.

Your work is saved

Answers are saved automatically in this browser. To continue on another device, click Save a backup file, then Open a backup file on the other device.

Where your resume is saved

Everything you type, and your photo, is saved only in this browser on this device. There is no account and no login.

What is sent to a server

Your resume content and photo are not sent to our server. We cannot see them.

Usage data

This version does not collect or send usage analytics.

Files loaded from other services

To show fonts and to create PDF and Word files, this page downloads fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from jsDelivr. Like any website, these services may receive your internet (IP) address and basic connection details. They do not receive your resume.

How to delete your data

Press Delete all data on this device. This removes your answers, photo and settings from this browser. Backup files you saved yourself are not affected; delete them from your Downloads folder.
