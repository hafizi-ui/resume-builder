Checklist & tips

CheckStatusTip to improve

1. Fill in your details

On Fill-In Form, type into the yellow boxes. The grey tip under each box shows an example. Required boxes, including email, are marked *.

2. Check your resume

Open Resume to see the full A4 page. Then open Resume Coach and aim for a score of 80 or more before you send it.

3. Add your photo

Click Upload photo at the top of the form. If you prefer no photo, untick “Show photo box on resume”.

4. Extra slots

A 2nd job, a 3rd leadership role and a 4th achievement appear on the resume as soon as you fill them in. Empty lines are always hidden.

5. Download

Download PDF gives a one-page A4 file that recruiters and ATS systems can read. Download Word gives an editable .docx file.

Your work is saved

Answers are saved automatically in this browser. To continue on another device, click Save a backup file, then Open a backup file on the other device.
